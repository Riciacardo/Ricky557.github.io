My Story

Hey everyone! I'm a passionate 3D builder, and environment artist with 3+ years of experience in Roblox Studio. I specialize in crafting immersive environments, detailed assets, and optimized game worlds that enhance gameplay and visual appeal. My focus is on realism, performance, and creativity, ensuring every scene feels alive and engaging.

Whether it's designing vast landscapes, intricate structures, or atmospheric lighting, I strive for high-quality results while maintaining efficiency.

The delivery time depends on the size of the project, but I always aim for efficiency and quality—at most, it takes me one week to complete a commission (Please note, this is a general estimate, but delivery time varies from project to project). Excited to collaborate and bring unique ideas to life!

Commission Terms

  • Payment: 50% upfront and 50% after completion. No refunds after final delivery.
  • Revisions: Small adjustments are unlimited. Major changes or restyles will require extra payment.
  • Timeframe: Delivery time depends on project size — I’ll always provide an estimate before starting.
  • Communication: Please be clear and responsive.
  • Delivery time: 1-4 weeks (variable)
  • Project rejection: I may refuse or terminate a project if communication, payment, or behavior becomes unprofessional, with no refund if work has already begun.
  • Goal: I aim to deliver optimized, detailed, and original builds — made to fit your vision as closely as possible.
